Gordon's Legacy

This project carries on the work of RGF, the author of Gordon's Reloading Tool, who gave it freely to the reloading community. We are grateful for everything he accomplished. The credit for GRT, its model and its ideas is his, and that of the volunteers who kept it running after him; we take none of it. What we add is an open, lasting home for that work and, where measurements and the published science support it, careful attempts to improve on it.

This software simulates. It does not produce load data. Check every charge against published data from the powder or bullet maker, start low, and work up while watching for pressure signs.
